
# My Experience Working at ALDO

### 1. What was it that you did?
I worked as a Retail Associate at ALDO. My responsibilities included helping customers find shoes and accessories, answering questions, organizing merchandise, and assisting with sales. I also worked with other employees to keep the store running smoothly.

### 2. When did you do it?
I worked at ALDO from October 2021 to June 2024.

### 3. What was good about it?
What I enjoyed most was interacting with different customers and helping them find what they were looking for. When I first started, I wasn't always comfortable approaching people, but over time I became more confident. I also learned how important communication and patience are when working with customers.

### 4. What was your favourite memory of it?
One of my favourite parts was helping customers find something they really liked. It felt good when someone came into the store unsure of what they wanted but left happy with their purchase. It made me feel like I was actually making their shopping experience better.

### 5. What didn't you enjoy about it?
The busiest days could be stressful, especially when several customers needed help at the same time. It wasn't always easy to balance helping customers, organizing the store, and keeping up with other tasks.

### 6. Give your experience a letter grade
**Grade: B**

I would give this experience a B because I enjoyed most of my time working at ALDO. It helped me become more confident, improve my communication skills, and learn how to work with different types of people. Even though there were stressful days, I gained experience that I can use in future jobs.
