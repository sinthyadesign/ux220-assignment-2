
# My Experience at Frontline Community Centre

### 1. What was it that you did?
I worked as an Assistant Secretary and Receptionist at Frontline Community Centre. My responsibilities included answering phone calls, greeting visitors, helping with administrative tasks, organizing information, and assisting people who came into the centre. I also helped make sure things were organized and running smoothly.

### 2. When did you do it?
I worked at Frontline Community Centre from July 2020 to August 2024.

### 3. What was good about it?
One thing I liked about this experience was getting the chance to help different people. I learned how to communicate professionally, stay organized, and handle different responsibilities. It also helped me become more comfortable speaking with people and dealing with situations that I hadn't experienced before.

### 4. What was your favourite memory of it?
My favourite part was being able to interact with people who came into the centre and knowing that I was helping them in some way. I enjoyed being someone they could approach when they needed information or assistance.

### 5. What didn't you enjoy about it?
Sometimes there were multiple things that needed my attention at once. Answering calls, helping visitors, and completing administrative work could become overwhelming, especially during busier times. It taught me that managing my time and staying organized were important.

### 6. Give your experience a letter grade
**Grade: A**

I would give this experience an A because it helped me grow both personally and professionally. I gained experience with communication, organization, and working with different people. These are skills that I know will be useful in my future career.
