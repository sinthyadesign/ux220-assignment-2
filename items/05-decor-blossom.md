
# My Experience Starting Decor Blossom

### 1. What was it that you did?
I started working on my own small business idea called Decor Blossom. I wanted to create something where I could combine creativity with products that people could purchase for special occasions. Some of the ideas I've worked on include flower bouquets, custom rhinestone bottles, and personalized gifts. I've had to think about product ideas, materials, costs, presentation, and how I would promote my products.

### 2. When did you do it?
I started developing the idea for Decor Blossom in 2026.

### 3. What was good about it?
The best part about this experience is being able to work on something that is my own. I enjoy coming up with different ideas, choosing colours, and thinking about how I want the products to look. It has also made me think more about the business side of things, such as pricing, expenses, and what customers might be interested in buying.

### 4. What was your favourite memory of it?
One of my favourite parts has been working on the custom rhinestone bottle idea. Seeing how a regular bottle can be transformed into something more decorative and personalized made me excited about what I could create. It made the business idea feel more real instead of just something I had thought about.

### 5. What didn't you enjoy about it?
One challenge has been figuring out how much everything costs and how to price products without spending more than I can afford. Finding materials and making sure the final products look the way I imagined can also take time. Since I'm still developing the business, there is a lot I need to learn.

### 6. Give your experience a letter grade
**Grade: A**

I would give this experience an A because I enjoy being creative and having the freedom to develop my own ideas. Even though I am still learning about running a business, it has helped me become more independent and think about turning something I enjoy into an opportunity.
