
# My Experience Studying UX Design at Laurier

### 1. What was it that you did?
I started studying User Experience (UX) Design at Wilfrid Laurier University. Throughout the program, I've been learning about how people interact with websites, apps, and different types of technology. I've also worked on assignments involving interface design, research, personas, presentations, and understanding what makes a digital experience easy to use.

### 2. When did you do it?
I started my UX Design studies at Wilfrid Laurier University in 2026 and I am currently working towards completing my degree.

### 3. What was good about it?
What I enjoy about this program is that it allows me to be creative while also learning practical skills. I like exploring different apps and websites and thinking about how they could be improved. I've also learned that UX design involves more than making something look good. It's important to understand the people who will actually be using it.

### 4. What was your favourite memory of it?
One of my favourite parts has been working on design assignments and seeing my ideas turn into something visual. I enjoy being able to experiment with layouts, colours, and different design concepts. It's satisfying when I finally get something to look the way I imagined.

### 5. What didn't you enjoy about it?
One challenging part has been managing multiple assignments and deadlines at the same time. Some projects take longer than expected, especially when I am still learning how to use certain tools or figure out how to complete something. Balancing schoolwork with commuting has also been difficult.

### 6. Give your experience a letter grade
**Grade: B**

I would give this experience a B because I enjoy learning about UX design and developing my creative skills. It can be challenging at times, but I feel like I'm learning more about the career I want to pursue and the type of work I would like to do in the future.
